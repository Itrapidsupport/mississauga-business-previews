The lounge

Cocktails, shareable plates and music, late into the night.

The kitchen sends out wings, nachos, tacos, flatbreads on a charcoal crust, pastas and jerk chicken, plus a few showpieces like 24K wings finished with edible gold flakes. Vegetarian and vegan options are available.

The bar mixes its own signature cocktails next to the classics, pours wine by the glass or bottle, and runs full bottle service with two complimentary pitchers of mix.

Everyone is welcome here. Fume is LGBTQ+ friendly and rated 4.2 on Google.
